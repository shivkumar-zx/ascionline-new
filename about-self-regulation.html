About Self-Regulation

Advertising self-regulation is a system in which the advertising industry agrees to follow common standards and take responsibility for the ads it creates and carries. In India, ASCI helps make this system work through the ASCI Code and an independent process for reviewing advertising concerns.

The aim is simple: Advertising should be honest, truthful, decent, safe and fair, and should not mislead or harm consumers.

How does self-regulation work?

Advertisers, advertising agencies, media organisations and others involved in advertising are expected to follow the ASCI Code. If an ad does not meet these standards, ASCI can review it and recommend that it is changed or withdrawn.

Pre-Production Advice

ASCI also offers advertising advice before an ad is released, helping businesses identify possible issues early and get their advertising right from the start.

Why does self-regulation matter?

Responsible advertising gives us value and choice. It entertains and informs. It helps fund the media, sport and culture we enjoy so much. It can be a direct force for social good: encouraging us to contribute to good causes, discouraging us from drinking and driving. And it drives competition, provides jobs and powers the economy.

Indispensable Protection

Effective Ad Self-Regulation is indispensable because some advertising is irresponsible – misleading, harming or offending people. Self-regulation delivers fast, flexible and efficient rules at no cost to the taxpayer, ensuring a level playing field.

Self-regulation benefits everyone – consumers, industry, policymakers, and society

For consumers

It gives people a free and accessible way to raise concerns about advertisements with an independent body.

For the advertising industry

Common standards help businesses understand what is expected of them, support fair competition and build trust in advertising.

For policymakers

Self-regulation adds another layer of consumer protection alongside existing laws and can help address advertising concerns quickly.

For society

Responsible advertising helps reduce misleading, harmful or offensive communication and encourages advertisers to consider their wider impact.

⚖️ Working Alongside the Law

Self-regulation works alongside the law. It does not replace the role of government authorities or other regulators.

🤝 A Shared Responsibility

Responsible advertising is a shared responsibility. By following common standards and addressing problems when they arise, the industry can help keep advertising fair and trustworthy for consumers.
