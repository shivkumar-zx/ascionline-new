About ASCI

The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI), established in 1985, is a voluntary self-regulatory organisation committed to the cause of self-regulation in advertising. ASCI ensures the protection of consumer interests while maintaining fairness to the advertising industry.

Founded in 1985
Tripartite Industry Stewardship
Statutory Recognition Under Cable TV Act
EASA & ICAS Global Affiliate

Our Purpose
Est. 1985

Protecting Consumers. Supporting Responsible Industry.

The Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) works to protect consumers' interests while being fair to the advertising industry. We help keep ads in India honest, decent, safe and fair.

We were set up in 1985 with support from advertisers, advertising agencies, media organisations and businesses.

How we support responsible advertising:

We believe the advertising industry has a responsibility to ensure that the ads it creates and publishes meet high standards of honesty, fairness and safety. This commitment is at the heart of self-regulation.

✓

The ASCI Code: Self-regulatory guidelines providing guardrails that all advertising must adhere to, irrespective of where it appears.

✓

Training & Research: Helping advertisers understand and follow the ASCI Code and responsible advertising tenets through our training programs and research.

✓

Advertising Advice: Pre-production advisory service helping spot and address potential problems while an ad is being developed, before publication.

INDEPENDENCE • ACCOUNTABILITY • TRUST
Our Vision
Roadmap 2032

Vision 2032: Building & Measuring Ecosystem Trust

As advertising evolves, so does ASCI's role in supporting a responsible and trustworthy advertising ecosystem.

Through Vision 2032, ASCI aims to go beyond addressing concerns about individual advertisements to actively building, measuring and strengthening trust across India's advertising ecosystem.

Guiding the future of advertising:

This vision will guide our efforts to bring together industry stakeholders, encourage responsible innovation and develop new approaches to the challenges and opportunities shaping the future of advertising.

★

Stakeholder Unity: Aligning advertisers, agencies, media platforms, and consumers under collective responsibility.

★

Responsible Innovation: Developing proactive frameworks for generative AI, influencer marketing, and emerging media.

★

Ecosystem Trust Metrics: Moving from reactive case-handling to continuously measuring trust and transparency across India.

RESPONSIBILITY • INNOVATION • ECOSYSTEM TRUST

About Self-Regulation

Explore More

Advertising self-regulation is a system in which the advertising industry agrees to follow common standards and take responsibility for the ads it creates and carries.

For consumers

It gives people a free and accessible way to raise concerns about advertisements with an independent body.

For the advertising industry

Common standards help businesses understand what is expected of them, support fair competition and build trust in advertising.

For policymakers

Self-regulation adds another layer of consumer protection alongside existing laws and can help address advertising concerns quickly.

For society

Responsible advertising helps reduce misleading, harmful or offensive communication and encourages advertisers to consider their wider impact.

Work We Do

Explore More

Anyone can raise a concern with ASCI about an advertisement they’ve seen at no cost. We accept complaints from consumers, consumer organisations, government bodies and the advertising industry about the content of advertising.

01

Reviewing Complaints

Every complaint is considered against the ASCI Code. The steps we take depend on the issue and how complex the case is.

  • 40-member Consumer Complaints Council
  • Independent Review Panel (4 retired High Court judges)
  • 53 technical experts across 25+ fields
  • TARA complaints management system
Explore Complaints Procedure
02

What We Cover

ASCI looks at the content of advertisements and promotions to review whether they follow the ASCI Code across TV, print, digital platforms, radio, outdoor, point-of-sale and packaging.

  • Misleading or untruthful
  • Indecent or offensive
  • Harmful or unsafe
  • Unfair to competitors
Explore Code Coverage
03

Our Proactive Monitoring

ASCI does not only act when someone complains about an ad. We also proactively scan for advertisements that may breach the ASCI Code.

  • National Advertising Monitoring Services (NAMS 2012)
  • Focus on digital media
  • Early identification of problematic ads
  • Modify or withdraw ads in breach

Tripartite Structure & Civil Society Quorum

ASCI's credibility relies upon its strict separation of powers. Operational management, institutional policy, and case adjudication are maintained across distinct, independent bodies:

Board of Governors

Composed of senior executives from advertisers, media networks, and advertising agencies who establish institutional policy, code updates, and financial governance.

View Board Members

Consumer Complaints Council

The decision-making heart of ASCI. By constitution, at least 50% of the CCC consists of eminent civil society members—doctors, lawyers, consumer activists, and educators.

View CCC Roster

Independent Review Panel

Presided over by retired High Court Judges to independently hear appeals and procedural reviews, guaranteeing natural justice for both complainants and advertisers.

Key Milestones: 1985 to 2026

View Full Timeline

ASCI was set up in 1985 by people working in advertising and media to help keep ads in India decent, fair and honest. Since then, we have adapted our work as advertising, media and consumer expectations have changed. Here are some of the key milestones.

1985 – 2004 Foundations

Founding & CCC Adoption

ASCI was founded on 21 October 1985 by 43 members from the advertising and media industry. The ASCI Code became part of the codes of Doordarshan and All India Radio, and the Consumer Complaints Council (CCC) process was adopted.

2005 – 2014 Recognition

Recognition & NAMS Launch

The Government of India formally recognised ASCI as an advertising self-regulatory organisation. We launched NAMS to check ads without waiting for complaints, and began online learning for professionals.

2015 – 2020 Digital Reach

Digital Checks & Agreements

We expanded online checks to 3,000 digital platforms, published real-money gaming guidelines, and signed agreements with FSSAI (2015) and the Ministry of AYUSH (2016), with recognition from IRDAI and courts.

2021 – 2026 40 Years & AI

ASCI Academy, AI & Dark Patterns

Launched ASCI Academy, published guidelines for influencer advertising, AI-generated content, dark patterns, and greenwashing, marking 40 years of ASCI and hosting the ICAS Global Summit in Mumbai.
